## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a motivating, supportive, and invigorating digital companion for personal health and nutritional wellness. It balances scientific clarity with lifestyle vitality. 

### Emotional Response
The interface must feel optimistic, empowering, and clean—eradicating the clinical guilt or tedious chore often associated with calorie and macro logging. Data visualizations and micro-interactions should celebrate daily consistency and positive habit loops.

### Target Audience
Active individuals, wellness seekers, and mindful eaters who prioritize nutrition, fitness goals, and holistic health. They demand frictionless data entry on the go and crystal-clear data hierarchy when reviewing daily metrics.

### Visual Style
A blend of **Modern Minimalist Surface Architecture** with **Tactile, Vibrant Micro-Accents**:
- Clean, open, high-legibility canvas layers that elevate scannability.
- Rich, dimensional purples create a focused, premium foundation.
- Electric sunny ambers and warm golden accents act as active triggers, high-value highlights, and progress milestones.
- Smooth, friendly corners and pill-shaped badges soften data density and invite touch interactions.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-contrast tonal relationships designed for immediate macronutrient comprehension and effortless meal audit trails.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#6C2BD9` - Vivid Royal Purple):** Represents core brand strength, primary interactive states, focus headers, and completed states. Tint variations scale into deep violet (`#3B0764`) for high-contrast typography and radiant lavender (`#EDE9FE`) for container fills.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B` - Sunny Amber / Golden Yellow):** Injects dynamic warmth. Reserved for high-value metrics, streak indicators, active progress bars, primary action buttons, and calorie-limit thresholds.
- **Tertiary (`#A78BFA` - Radiant Lavender):** Softens the visual intensity, serving as secondary data charts, inactive rings, subtle surface tinting, and supporting borders.
- **Neutral (`#1E1B2E` - Deep Obsidian Ink):** Replaces pure black with an ultra-deep violet-charcoal to deliver high-contrast text and robust readability without stark digital harshness.
- **Backgrounds & Surfaces:** Canvas sits on `#FAF9FD`, moving up to crisp `#FFFFFF` for elevated modular cards and `#F3F0FA` for sunken input fields.

### Macronutrient Token Roles
- **Calories:** Golden Amber (`#F59E0B`)
- **Protein:** Deep Violet (`#6C2BD9`)
- **Carbohydrates:** Vibrant Aqua-Blue (`#0EA5E9`)
- **Fats:** Rose Coral (`#F43F5E`)

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** powers the entire design system, providing a clean, contemporary grotesque backbone with humanist geometric warmth. Its rounded terminals and open apertures maximize legibility in glanceable health tracking environments.

### Typography Hierarchy & Rules
- **Display & Metrics:** Built with extra-bold weights (`800`) to celebrate quantifiable achievements (e.g., total calories consumed, gram counts).
- **Headlines:** Form clear section groupings for meal categories (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks). Set tight line heights to maintain compact mobile card headers.
- **Labels & Badges:** `label-caps` uses all-caps styling with `+0.05em` letter spacing for macro indicators (`CAL`, `PRO`, `CARB`, `FAT`).
- **Tabular Figures:** For all metric displays and numerical tracking rows, enable font feature settings `'tnum' on` to prevent layout jittering during number animations or live recalculations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built mobile-first, ensuring rapid one-thumb data entry while gracefully expanding into an information-dense dashboard across larger viewports.

### Grid & Layout Structure
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column vertical stream. 4-column layout fluid grid with `0.75rem` gutters and `1rem` safe margins. Key actions, quick-add buttons, and bottom navigation stay pinned within the thumb zone.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins. Metrics ring and daily goal summary lock into a persistent sidebar or top row, with meal logging expanding into a dual-column card format.
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout centered with max container width of `1200px`. The dashboard bifurcates: 7 columns dedicated to progressive meal streams and time-based logging; 5 columns dedicated to sticky nutrient analytics, hydration gauges, and trend reports.

### Rhythm Principles
Component spacing follows an 8pt mathematical rhythm (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 36px). Dense data elements like nutrient breakdowns within table rows lock strictly to `space-xs` and `space-sm` gaps, while section dividers use `space-lg`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined blend of **soft ambient tinted shadows** and **tonal layer progression** to keep food tracking clean, tactile, and distinct.

### Tonal Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Tinted soft neutral (`#FAF9FD`). Non-interactive backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Module Containers & Default Cards):** Pure white surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a delicate lavender-tinted drop shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(108, 43, 217, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Floating Menus):** Pure white surface elevated with `0 10px 30px -4px rgba(108, 43, 217, 0.12)`. Applied to active meal cards, open bottom sheets, and dropdown selectors.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Key Quick-Action Triggers):** Prominent ambient lift: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(30, 27, 46, 0.18)`. Floating action buttons use a vibrant accent glow: `0 8px 24px 0 rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`.

### Ghost Separation
Cards and structural modules utilize an ultra-subtle `1px` border using `#F1EDFA` to guarantee separation even under direct outdoor sunlight or on uncalibrated mobile screens.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a welcoming, approachable roundedness level (`roundedness: 2`). This softens health tracking data and fosters a relaxed, modern feel.

### Shape Scale Applications
- **Base Components (`rounded`: 0.5rem / 8px):** Form input fields, checkbox containers, inline quantity steppers, and nutrient progress track bars.
- **Interactive Cards (`rounded-lg`: 1rem / 16px):** Meal cards (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks), macro summary widgets, and collapsible meal entries.
- **Prominent Surfaces (`rounded-xl`: 1.5rem / 24px):** Quick-log bottom sheets, dashboard summary banners, and full-screen modals.
- **Micro-Badges & Action Pills (Pill-shaped):** Macronutrient chips, status badges, tag selectors, and main CTA buttons use full pill rounding (`9999px`) to emphasize touch-friendliness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Log / Save):** Warm golden yellow background (`#F59E0B`), text `#1E1B2E` (Semi-bold), pill-rounded (`9999px`), with horizontal padding `space-lg` and vertical `space-md`. Subtle hover state deepens to `#D97706`.
- **Secondary (Add Food / New Meal):** Tinted lavender background (`#F3F0FA`), text `#6C2BD9` (Semi-bold), pill-rounded, zero shadow.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, text `#6C2BD9`, hover background `#FAF9FD`.

### Macronutrient Badge Chips
Pill-shaped compact tags (`padding: 4px 10px`) indicating individual nutritional values:
- **Calories:** Surface `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`, label text 10px bold uppercase.
- **Protein:** Surface `#EDE9FE`, text `#6C2BD9`, label text 10px bold uppercase.
- **Carbs:** Surface `#E0F2FE`, text `#0369A1`, label text 10px bold uppercase.
- **Fats:** Surface `#FFE4E6`, text `#BE123C`, label text 10px bold uppercase.

### Meal Logging Cards (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks)
- **Container:** Pure white card, `1rem` radius, 1px border `#F1EDFA`, shadow Level 1.
- **Header:** Meal title in `headline-sm`, total meal calories in `label-lg` with an amber highlight, and a distinct plus-circle button to trigger rapid entry.
- **Item Rows:** Separated by hairline borders (`#FAF9FD`). Item title, serving size (`body-sm` in `#6B7280`), and aligned right-hand macro breakdown. Swipe-left reveals edit/delete actions.

### Input Fields (Food Search, Serving Quantities)
- **Default State:** Height 48px, background `#F8F7FC`, border 1.5px solid `#E9E5F5`, radius 8px, text `#1E1B2E`.
- **Focus State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1.5px solid `#6C2BD9`, shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(108, 43, 217, 0.15)`.
- **Quick-Search Prepend:** Inline magnifying glass and trailing barcode scanner shortcut.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Radio Selectors
- **Selection Controls:** 20px squares with 6px rounded corners. Unchecked: 1.5px border `#D1D5DB`. Checked: Solid `#6C2BD9` fill with white checkmark icon.
- **Segmented Meal Switcher:** Full-width pill-shaped container (`#F3F0FA`) with animated white sliding pill selector to switch between *Today*, *Yesterday*, and *Custom Date*.

### Metric Rings & Progress Bars
- **Progress Track:** Flat light grey-purple base (`#EDE9FE`).
- **Indicator Fill:** Dynamic gradient or solid fills matching nutrient token keys with rounded cap ends. Over-budget calorie limits trigger warning state `#EF4444`.
